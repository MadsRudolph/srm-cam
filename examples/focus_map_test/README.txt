Laser focus map test board, 140 x 84 mm, top copper (not mirrored).

A 1 mm trace runs all the way round, 5 mm in from the edge, with a 1 mm cross
through the middle; short 0.25 mm traces sit near each corner and in the
middle. Etched on a clean copper sheet after a focus map, the isolation along
the edges should come out as clean as in the middle if the map is right:
a wrong height shows first as a wider, ragged or unfinished moat, and on the
0.25 mm traces.
