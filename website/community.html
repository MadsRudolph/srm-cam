Community & Discord

The SRM-CAM Discord is where to ask about making a board, on the mill or the laser, and get an answer from Mads or from students who have done it. New features are explained there first, in more detail than the release notes.

Join the Discord → Troubleshooting first

What is there

#announcementsNew releases and features, explained with pictures: what changed, and how to use it. #helpQuestions about a board, the mill, the laser or SRM-CAM. One thread per problem. #show-your-boardsPhotos of what you made: milled, lasered, masked. The best end up in this guide. #feature-requestsWhat SRM-CAM should do next. #bug-reportsSomething the app did wrong. Say what you pressed and what it said.

Asking a good question

  1. Look it up firstSearch Troubleshooting: the common problems are there with their fix.
  2. Say where you areThe board, the method (the chip at the top left), and the step on the rail.
  3. Show itA screenshot of the window, and a photo of the board or the machine. For the laser, laser.log helps; for a run, the run's folder under Documents\SRM-CAM\runs.
  4. Say what it saidThe exact message, copied, not remembered.
Personal support

Stuck at the machine with something the guide doesn't cover? Ask in #help and Mads will answer, or arrange to help in person.

Prefer a form? Feedback sends your comments straight to Mads.
