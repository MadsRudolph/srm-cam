Double-sided boards

Both sides register off machine-located holes — never the board edge. Tick Double-sided in 3 · Registration (needs F.Cu).

  • clr L / S: hole clearances — defaults are dialed in for this machine; only touch if a pin binds.

Sequence

  1. Read <name>_runplan.txt — it has the exact order, bits, pin sizes.
  2. Zero once — XY once for the whole board; Z after every bit change and the flip.
  3. Run _align — drills the dowel holes; seat the pins.
  4. Bottom — drill files, then _bottom_traces.
  5. Flip onto the pins — wrong direction won't seat.
  6. Top, then cutout — re-zero Z, _top_traces, _cutout last.

Fiducial holes

Settings: n fiducials (use 4), placement (On board / In waste / Manual), flip direction, optional scale fit.

After milling the bottom and flipping: Fit & export top... opens the alignment dialog. Fill in each fiducial's measured position:

WayHow
TypeJog by hand, read VPanel, type X/Y
CaptureJog the bit into the hole, press — DRO position fills in
AutoJog roughly over the hole, press — found electrically to ~0.05 mm, ~1 min

Compute fit shows RMS (want < 0.1 mm), rotation, scale. OK exports the warped top traces.

Auto finder requirements

  • Firmware v3 (reflash once with python scripts/flash_firmware.py), red clip on the copper, spindle OFF.
  • Jog within ~1 mm of the hole, 2–3 mm up.
  • Copper must exist ~2.5 mm west of the hole (surface reference touch).
Verify the flip direction before cutting the top

Corner fiducials cannot detect a wrong flip direction — the fit looks great either way. Before running top traces: click-to-jog to a distinctive board hole in the Top view and confirm the bit hovers over it in reality. Ten seconds, saves the board.

Level the top too

After the flip: View = Top, Build grid, Clear Z, re-probe, Export top traces (leveled). See Bed leveling.
