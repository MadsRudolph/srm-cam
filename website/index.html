SRM-CAM

Mill a PCB from KiCad on the Roland SRM-20. Load the Gerbers, push the copper into the corner, press Start.

Windows installer Linux AppImage Open source
Mill a board → Download for Windows Linux AppImage

How it works

  1. Load your boardOpen the Gerber folder from KiCad.
  2. Copper in the cornerSlide the sheet into the bracket and hold it down.
  3. Press StartIt finds the copper, levels, drills and cuts the traces by itself.
  4. Check itThe board comes forward so you can look, and recut anything shallow.
  5. Cut it outPress one button. Double-sided adds one flip in the middle.

Mill a board is the only page you need at the machine. New to the mill, or stuck? Ask Mads Rudolph or the course TAs.
