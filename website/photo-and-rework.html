Photo check & rework

Did every channel cut through? Box the spots that did not and re-cut them deeper, in one program. The board stays on the machine — never re-zero XY.

Rework

Rework is on the rail under When you need it (Full tier).

  1. Repeat from Isolation traces or Board cut-out — on a double-sided job, Bottom traces or Top traces. The geometry is the pass as it was cut, clipped to your boxes, so it follows the same route.
  2. Mark the spots Tick Drag boxes on the bed and drag a box over each spot. Each box keeps its own depth; set Depth for the next box before drawing, or edit it in the table. Go a little deeper than the pass that missed.
  3. Export the rework program… One <name>_<pass>_rework.nc. It is warped to the face's height map if one is applied, and a cut-out rework is taken down in passes rather than one deep plunge.

A photo of the board

A board that has been cut differs from its design: it sits a degree off, a trace lifted. A photo warped into machine coordinates puts the two in one frame, so a box drawn on the photo lands where the damage is.

  1. Shoot Straight down, evenly lit, sharp. View → Take one with a phone… shows a QR code; scan it, take the photo on the phone, and it arrives in the app.
  2. Lay it on the bed View → Lay a photo of the board on the bed…. Click the four anchor holes in the photo in the order the design map beside it asks for. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, undo the last click. The note says how well the fit lands.
  3. Look The photo is drawn under the work and the work is faded so the photo reads. View → Take the photo off when done.
Not in this interface

Automatic detection of uncut channels from the photo, probing the rework boxes, and choosing your own anchor holes are in the first interface (SRM-CAM --original).
