Troubleshooting

Symptom → fix. Learned on real boards.

Cutting

SymptomCause → fix
Head lifts to the top, cuts air or shallowDepth outside the ~60 mm Z travel → raise the work surface (add spoilboard). Diagnostics checks this.
Cuts through in some areas, not othersSurface not flat vs a 0.15 mm cut → level the bed. Already leveled? Run Mesh check — a flaky point poisons its neighborhood. Missed spots → rework + Probe boxes.
Red channels in the Traces previewGaps narrower than the bit → will short. Widen clearance in KiCad, smaller bit, or V-bit.
Wrong depth after a bit changeRe-zero Z after every bit change and after the flip. Never XY.

Probing & machine link

SymptomCause → fix
Connect failsArduino Serial Monitor open (close it), wrong COM port, or replug the Arduino.
NOTOUCHRed clip off / dirty copper / board not isolated from the bed / datum too high.
UNSTABLEFlaky contact: clean the copper, reseat the red clip.
RUNAWAYPoint over a hole / off the board / clip fell off. This guard saved your bed — fix and re-probe.
NODATUMStart probing from the app (it sets the datum for you).
ABORTYou pressed STOP or the lid opened. Nothing broken.
bit ○ never fillsTouch the red clip to the bit. Still nothing → check D7 at the Arduino + firmware banner in a serial terminal.
Spiky mesh in 3D viewFlaky touches. Mesh check flags them; re-probe those points.

Double-sided

SymptomCause → fix
Top mirrored/offset after the flipPhysical flip ≠ configured direction. Fiducial fits can't catch this. Prevention: jog to a distinctive board hole in Top view before cutting — bit must hover over it.
Large fiducial fit RMSRe-capture the worst fiducial (Auto is repeatable), clamp before measuring, use all 4 corners.
Auto finder failsStart within ~1 mm of the hole; needs copper 2.5 mm west of it; needs firmware v2 (reflash).
NC job ignores my originNC obeys G54 only, not the User CS. Set G54 in VPanel: XY at machine origin, zero only Z. Verify G54 Z once after Zero Z on machine.

Photo & rework

SymptomCause → fix
Detect from photo refusesPhoto can't answer: too low-res, soft focus, glare, or bad anchors. Reshoot closer/sharper/straighter, re-click anchors with the guide green.
Boxes over areas that look fineDelete false alarms — but look twice: extras cluster where the mesh under-measured.
Missed a spot you know is badShallow cuts look cut in any photo. Use Probe boxes + Mesh check for depth failures.
Overlay doesn't line upRe-do anchors (zoom in, watch the angle guide); shoot straighter down.

Sessions

SymptomCause → fix
Session loads, board missingSessions store the Gerber folder path — restore the folder or reload it; mesh/boxes/photo are intact in the file.
Can't find exports/sessionsDocuments\SRM-CAM\exports / ...\sessions. Dialogs reopen the last-used folder.
