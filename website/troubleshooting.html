Troubleshooting

Symptoms first, causes second. Most of these were learned the hard way on real boards — the fix listed is the one that actually worked.

Cutting problems

The head lifts to the top and cuts air (or cuts far too shallow)

Cause: the requested depth is outside the SRM-20's usable Z travel (~60 mm). When the work surface sits too low, the machine clamps at the top of its range and the "cut" happens above the board.

Fix: raise the work surface — add spoilboard under the stock — rather than fighting the origin. Run Diagnostics (with Z probed) before full-bed jobs; it checks exactly this.

Traces cut through in some areas but not others

Cause: the surface isn't flat relative to a ~0.15 mm cut — bowed stock, uneven bed, or a height map with a bad measurement in it.

Fix: probe a height map and export with leveling (Bed leveling). If you already leveled and still see failures, run Mesh check — a flaky probe point reads the surface lower than it is, and everything near it cuts shallow. For spots already missed: the rework flow, with Probe boxes to measure the true surface at each failed spot.

Red channels in the traces preview

Cause: copper gaps narrower than the bit — physically impossible to isolate; they will short.

Fix: widen the clearance in KiCad, use a smaller bit, or switch the trace tool to a V-bit with a smaller target width.

Depth is wrong after a bit change

Cause: forgot to re-zero Z. Every bit sits at a different stick-out.

Fix: re-zero Z after every bit change and after the flip. XY is never re-zeroed mid-board.

Probing & machine link

Connect fails / no response from the Arduino

  • The Arduino IDE's Serial Monitor must be closed — it holds the port exclusively.
  • Check you picked the Arduino's COM port, not another device.
  • Power-cycle the Arduino (replug USB) and reconnect.

Probe error meanings

ErrorMeaningWhat to do

NODATUMProbe command before a datum was setJog above grid point 1 and start the probe run from the app (it sends the datum for you).
ABORTYou pressed STOP (or the lid opened mid-move)Nothing broken — resolve the reason and re-run.

The touch indicator never fills

Short the two clips together with the app connected. If bit ○ still doesn't fill, the problem is wiring/firmware, not the board contact: check D7 and GND at the Arduino, and confirm the firmware banner in a serial terminal.

The mesh looks spiky in the 3D view

Real boards are smooth. Isolated spikes are flaky touches — run Mesh check to have them flagged, re-probe those points, and only then trust the map.

Double-sided registration

The top traces landed mirrored / offset after the flip

Cause (most likely): the physical flip direction didn't match the configured one. With corner fiducials the fit math cannot catch this — corner patterns fit well under both hypotheses.

Fix / prevention: before cutting the top, click-to-jog to a distinctive board hole in the Top view and confirm the bit hovers over that hole in reality. Ten seconds, saves a board.

Fiducial fit RMS is large

  • One measurement is bad — re-capture the fiducial whose residual is largest (use Auto for a repeatable measurement).
  • The board moved between measurements — clamp first, then measure.
  • Fiducials too close together — spread drives fit accuracy; use all four corners.

The Auto fiducial finder fails or reports copper where the hole is

  • Start position too far off — jog within ~1 mm of the hole center first.
  • It probes copper ~2.5 mm west of the hole to learn the surface; make sure there's copper there (not another hole or the board edge).
  • Needs firmware v2 — reflash srm20_spi_probe.ino if Auto reports a firmware error. See Hardware & firmware.

NC job ignores the origin I set on the machine

NC (G-code) jobs obey G54 only. The origin shown in VPanel's user coordinate system is not automatically G54 on every machine config. Set the G54 origin in VPanel explicitly: keep XY at the machine origin and zero only Z. After using Zero Z on machine, verify VPanel's G54 Z once before trusting it for NC work.

Photo & rework

Detect from photo refuses to answer

That's it protecting you from garbage boxes. Causes, in order of likelihood: photo resolution too low for the channel width (get closer — the detector needs the channels well-resolved), soft focus over part of the board, glare washing out the copper, or misclicked anchors. Reshoot straight-down, sharp, evenly lit; re-click the anchors carefully with the angle guide green.

Detect from photo proposes boxes over areas that look fine

Review and delete — false alarms happen, especially in areas where the photo is blurry or glare-y. But before deleting, look twice: extras sometimes cluster in zones the height map under-measured, where cuts are genuinely marginal.

It didn't flag a spot I know is bad

If the channel was cut but too shallow, it looks cut in any photo — that failure class is invisible optically. Use Probe boxes to measure the real surface at suspect spots, and Mesh check to find where the map was wrong.

The photo overlay doesn't line up with the design

Re-do the anchors (Load photo... again): zoom right in on each hole before clicking, and watch the angle guide — it turns red when a click can't belong to the rectangle you're building. A photo shot at an angle also stretches the fit; shoot as straight-down as possible.

Sessions & app

Session loads but the board is missing

Sessions store the Gerber folder path. If the folder moved (or the session came from another PC), restore the folder or reload it manually — the rest of the session (mesh, boxes, photo) is intact inside the file.

Where did my exports/sessions go?

Documents\SRM-CAM\exports and Documents\SRM-CAM\sessions. Dialogs remember the last-used folder, so if you saved elsewhere once, the dialog will reopen there.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub with the symptom, the runplan step you were on, and (for probing issues) the exact error line from the status bar.
